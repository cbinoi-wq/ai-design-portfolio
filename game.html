Week 3 · Test, refine, and publish

From a static draft to a living dream.

This final pass focused on making growth, deception, and movement readable without losing the soft visual style.

01

Feedback I focused on

Playtesters liked the cute creature, but its growth was hard to notice. The false objects appeared too late, and both the character and city felt too static.

02

What changed

False copies now shimmer from Stage 1 and reveal themselves when touched. The creature grows in five visible steps, and the city moves with brighter stardust.

03

AI and my decisions

I used ChatGPT to generate a transparent six-pose character sprite sheet and help refine the canvas animation, interaction code, responsive layout, and testing. I chose the concept, rules, visual direction, pacing, feedback priorities, and final design edits.
