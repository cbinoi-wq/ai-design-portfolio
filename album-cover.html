01 · Album cover redesign

UNETHICAL

Faouzia · Dark romantic visual identity

The song feels vulnerable, emotionally trapped, and quietly powerful. I translated that tension into a bouquet of soft ivory and wine-red flowers wrapped too tightly in delicate black lace.

Listen to the song ↗
Mood

Intimate, elegant, tense

Visual language

Flowers, black lace, deep shadow

Audience

Listeners drawn to emotional pop

Tools

AI image generation + layout refinement

Process

Finding the right metaphor.

The first ideas were more literal: a broken heart, a red thread, and a fractured reflection. Each test helped narrow the concept until the flowers and lace expressed the emotion without looking like a movie poster.

Final decision

“The flowers feel beautiful and vulnerable. The lace looks delicate, but it also crosses and tightens around the petals.”

The restrained typography and open black space let the image stay dominant and remain readable at thumbnail size. The finished cover feels emotional and elegant instead of frightening.
